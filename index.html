Slide 3 • High-Margin Field Scope #1

Detailed Site Assessments & 2-Person NDT/Material Testing

Tasks 03 & 05

1. Detailed Site Assessments (Task 05, Item 3)

When Triggered:

Assigned when a bridge lacks plans, shop drawings, or dimension records needed for load rating.

Field Execution (2-Person Crew):

  • Review ProjectWise bridge files & BrM history.
  • Coordinate District traffic control / lane closures.
  • Measure full geometry (spans, beams, section loss).
  • Drone Authorization: RFP Page 12 explicitly permits using drones for Site Assessments.

Required Deliverables:

  • Schematic Drawings: Simple CAD line drawings for permanent SCDOT files.
  • Data Correction Form: File PE-signed QC package in ProjectWise.

2. Non-Destructive & Material Testing (Tasks 03 & 05)

Built for "2-Person Teams" (RFP Page 15):

  • GPR Scanning: Locate rebar spacing, cover & shear studs. Estimated at 30 mins/loc (4 locs/bridge).
  • Rebound Hammer: 7 readings at multiple locations to test concrete compressive strength.
  • Core Sampling & Lab Testing: Extract cores, patch holes, and run lab compression tests to eliminate load postings.
  • Reinforcement Excavation: Demo concrete, measure rebar size/spacing on planless bridges, and patch.
  • Steel NDT (UT / MP / DP): Ultrasonic thickness gauges + Magnetic Particle & Dye Penetrant crack testing.

Required Deliverables:

  • Pre-work Testing/Sampling Plan (SCDOT BMO approval).
  • Testing Report with lab results uploaded to ProjectWise.
Slide 4 • High-Volume Field Scope #2

Emergency Storm Response (Task 04) & NBIS Overflow (Task 02)

Tasks 02 & 04

3. Storm, Scour & Collision Inspections (Task 04)

  • 800 Bridges/Month Emergency Volume (Page 6): Negotiated as contract modifications during severe weather.
  • 24-Hour Storm Response: Mobilize within 24 hours for groups of bridges to check structural elements, soundings, channel evaluations, and signs.
  • 4-Hour Collision Response: For vehicle/vessel strikes, a registered South Carolina PE (Structural) must respond on-site within 4 hours.
  • 24/7 On-Call POCs: Primary and secondary contacts.
  • Deliverables: Verbal/written Preliminary Report ASAP; finalized QC-reviewed Report in BrM/ProjectWise within 2 weeks.

4. Routine NBIS Inspections & BrM Support (Task 02)

  • 350 Routine Bridges/Month Quota: Primes sub out geographic clusters to keep pace with monthly schedules.
  • Field Execution: Element-level NBIS inspections per SCDOT BIGD and federal SNBI rules.
  • Railroad & District Coordination: Coordinate District traffic control and railroad flagging/Right-of-Entry (8 months prior).
  • Deliverables: Monthly tracking spreadsheets (Asset ID, bridge category SE3/SB2, lane closure modifiers) + finalized reports in BrM and ProjectWise.
