Slide 3 • High-Margin Field Scope #1

Detailed Site Assessments & 2-Person NDT/Material Testing

Tasks 03 & 05 (Pages 10, 12–15)

1. Detailed Site Assessments (Task 05, Item 3)

When Triggered:

Assigned whenever a bridge has missing plans, no shop drawings, or unquantified deterioration preventing a load rating.

Field Execution (2-Person Crew):

  • Review existing ProjectWise bridge files & BrM history prior to mobilization.
  • Coordinate with SCDOT District Office for lane closures / traffic control.
  • Measure full bridge geometry (spans, beams, substructure, section loss).
  • Drone Authorization: RFP Page 12 explicitly permits using drones for Detailed Site Assessments without hands-on access.

Required Deliverables:

  • Schematic Drawings: Simple CAD line drawings documenting all field dimensions for permanent SCDOT bridge files.
  • Data Correction Form: Update BrM data and file PE-signed QC package in ProjectWise.

2. Non-Destructive & Material Testing (Tasks 03 & 05)

Explicitly Built for a "2-Person Team" (RFP Page 15):

  • NDT – Ground Penetrating Radar (GPR): Locate rebar spacing, cover & shear studs on concrete bridges/culverts. Estimated at 30 mins/location (4 locations/bridge = 2 hrs scanning).
  • NDT – Rebound Hammer: Record 7 readings at multiple locations along bridge decks to test concrete compressive strength.
  • Concrete Core Sampling & Lab Testing: Pull field cores (and steel rebar coupons), patch holes, and run lab compressive strength tests to eliminate bridge load postings.
  • Reinforcement Excavation: Demo localized concrete, measure rebar size/spacing on planless bridges, and patch concrete.
  • Steel NDT (UT / MP / DP): Ultrasonic thickness gauges (corrosion severity) + Magnetic Particle & Dye Penetrant crack testing on steel members, pins, eyebars, and gusset plates.

Required Deliverables:

  • Pre-work Testing/Sampling Plan (requires SCDOT BMO approval).
  • Draft & Final NDT/Material Report with locations, lab results, and recommended material properties uploaded to ProjectWise.
Slide 4 • High-Volume Field Scope #2

Emergency Storm Response (Task 04) & NBIS Overflow (Task 02)

Tasks 02 & 04 (Pages 6, 8–11)

3. Storm, Scour & Collision Inspections (Task 04)

  • 800 Bridges/Month Emergency Volume (Page 6): Negotiated as a contract modification during hurricanes, floods, winter storms, or earthquakes.
  • 24-Hour Storm Response: Mobilize within 24 hours for groups of bridges to perform above-water structural checks, soundings, channel evaluations, and overhead sign/signal inspections.
  • 4-Hour Collision Response: For single-bridge vehicle/vessel strikes, a registered South Carolina PE (Structural) must respond on-site within 4 hours (and load raters within 8 hours).
  • 24/7 On-Call POCs: Primary and secondary contacts listed.
  • Deliverables: Verbal/written Preliminary Report ASAP; finalized QC-reviewed Emergency Inspection Report in BrM and ProjectWise within 2 weeks.

4. Routine NBIS Inspections & BrM Support (Task 02)

  • 350 Routine Bridges/Month Quota: Primes sub out geographic clusters or culvert/bridge packages to keep pace with monthly schedules.
  • Field Execution: Element-level NBIS inspections per SCDOT BIGD and federal SNBI rules.
  • Railroad & District Coordination: Coordinate District traffic control (night/weekend lane closures on interstates) and railroad flagging/Right-of-Entry (8 months in advance).
  • Deliverables: Monthly tracking spreadsheets (Asset ID, bridge category SE3/SB2, lane closure modifiers) + finalized reports in BrM and ProjectWise (all prior-year reports finalized by March 1).
